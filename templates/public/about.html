Welcome to Fahad's Tutorial - FT

As a premier "School of Digital Education" and one of Bangladesh's leading Online Education Platforms, FT is dedicated to nurturing the smart citizens of our country and beyond.

Our Story & Vision

Established in 2019, FT is registered as a proprietorship venture under the stewardship of Md. Fahad Hossain. Led by our CEO and a dedicated management team, we tirelessly work towards realizing the Vision 2041 of our nation, with a special focus on transforming the education sector into a hub of innovation and excellence.

Our team of professional and experienced teachers is committed to providing comprehensive academic and professional support through online courses and personalized offline programs. Whether it's general education, technical skills, soft skills, or knowledge building, FT offers tailored courses to meet the diverse needs of students, parents, teachers, and professionals both domestically and internationally.

Visionary Leadership

"Under the visionary leadership of Mr. Fahad Hossain, FT has made significant strides in positively impacting the education sector. We firmly believe that our meaningful contributions have played a pivotal role in shaping the future of education, and we are committed to continuing this journey with dedication and enthusiasm."

With a team of mentors, teachers, and staff fully dedicated to our mission, we are confident in our ability to realize our aspirations and elevate Fahad's Tutorial – FT to new heights within the global education landscape.
